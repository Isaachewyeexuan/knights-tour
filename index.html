Knight's Tour

Audio Controls
Click any square to start.
(Scroll=Pan, Ctrl+Scroll=Zoom Board, Shift+Scroll=Pan H)

HOW TO PLAY:

  1. Select the board size and click 'Set Size / Restart'.
  2. Click any square to place your first Knight.
  3. Click on the green highlighted squares to move in an "L" shape.
  4. The goal is to visit every square on the board exactly once!
  5. Tip: You can click on any previously visited square's number to Undo back to that exact move.

HISTORY:

The Knight's Tour is a classic mathematical problem dating back to the 9th century in Arabic manuscripts. It was extensively analyzed by the famous mathematician Leonhard Euler in 1759. Finding a full path is a classic example of computing a Hamiltonian path in graph theory.
